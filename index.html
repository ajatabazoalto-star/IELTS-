Part 2 Cue Card (2 minutes)

Describe a health problem you or someone you know experienced.

You should say:

  • What the problem was and how it started
  • How it was diagnosed or treated
  • How long the recovery took
  • And explain how this experience changed your view on health.

💡 Try to use at least 5 of these words: diagnose, administer, admit, discharge, examine, infection, disease, dehydration, stroke, obesity, allergy, addiction, cancer.
